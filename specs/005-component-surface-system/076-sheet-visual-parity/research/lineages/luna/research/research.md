## Eliminated alternatives and divergence map

The following are explicitly ruled out:

- Nested executor dispatch or an ad hoc loop: the user’s detached-lineage contract and the deep-research executor invariant require one inline iteration at a time.
- Early synthesis after convergence telemetry: the hard max takes precedence and all five iterations were mandatory.
- Treating a differently named provenance field as D9-compliant Source metadata: provenance without selected source, reason, rung, and state is not traceable.
- Keeping card targets and relying on a late CREATE override: D7 is a target ruling and a green lane must not certify a prohibited container.
- Rewriting 012 to ClickUp: the board rule is scoped; 012’s Anytype single-column field layout is an intentional exception.
- Letting the token list alone be the design system: row, divider, header, stack, and status behavior remains consumer-specific without shared primitive ownership.
- Treating the iteration guard as a liveness detector: it counts iterations and does not prove that a non-human phase is progressing.
- Letting two model JUDGE passes close a child: D6 keeps the operator device read as the final closure gate.
