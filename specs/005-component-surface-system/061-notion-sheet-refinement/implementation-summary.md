## Known Limitations

1. **No Notion capture shows a multi-cell selection in a table.** None exists in the 3,647-file
   harvest. The single-row bar is therefore **our** shape at Notion's measured density, and it is
   labelled as such rather than claimed as parity.
2. **Notion contributes shape and arrangement only.** Every iOS file is a 299×678-680px thumbnail
   with named rather than sampled colours, so no pixel, colour, scrim or timing value comes from
   this source. The 16px card inset is a floor read proportionally, not a parity figure.
3. **Two rows cannot be closed from this repository.** AC-005 is the operator's device read and
   AC-007 waits on an Anytype capture re-read the operator schedules.
4. **Notion has no dark-theme capture for this surface at all.** Zero dark sheet, menu or dialog
   files exist in the harvest, so no dark-theme Notion claim is possible — a gap on Notion's side,
   not ours.
