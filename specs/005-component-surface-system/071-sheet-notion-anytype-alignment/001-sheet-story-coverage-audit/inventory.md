## How to read this table

One row per surface the app can open (sheet, panel, popover, menu card, toast). No later phase in
`071` may claim coverage without a row here naming it (packet decision D1). Cells:

- **Producer** — the `src/` call site that opens the surface (file:line at time of audit).
- **Phone** — how it presents on phone: `sheet` (bottom), `popover`, `modal`, `panel`, `toast`.
- **Story?** — yes / allowlisted (exempt, in `tools/storybook/story-coverage-allowlist.json`) / NO.
- **Captures** — screenshot-manifest capture ids, or `none`.
- **Notion / Anytype** — reference capture(s) under `screenshots/notion/` and `screenshots/anytype/`,
  or `none` (recorded, never silently skipped).
- **First-read gap** — what the references show that the app does not yet, on first read.

Counts are asserted in a test; regenerate rows with `node tools/storybook/sheet-inventory.mjs`.
