## What Was Built

The question "do we truly have screenshots and stories for every sheet?" now has a
single answer you can regenerate instead of trust: 86 surfaces, counted, named, and
pinned by a test that fails when the honest answer changes.

### Phase 1: sheet-story-coverage-audit

`inventory.md` lists every surface the app can open — 54 primary (the 17 grammar-registered
plus 11 overflow-only surfaces, 18 unfolded modal producers plus the anonymous trash-restore
modal, the three suggest modals, the four view-toolbar option popovers, toast, column menu,
bulk-edit menu) and 32 stacked children — with the settings sheet and the add-property /
property-type picker sheet as rows 1-2. Each row names its producer call site, its phone
presentation, its story state (6 rows with stories, 5 allowlisted, the rest untracked with
the reason the coverage gate cannot see them), its capture ids (68 rows), and its
Notion/Anytype reference mapping or the recorded absence (46 rows have none on either side;
22 cite Notion, 33 cite Anytype). The coverage census itself is 40 = 19 stories + 21
allowlisted, 0 missing, 0 stale — no gap required a story, so every find is a recorded
absence carried into the later phases' rows, exactly the decision the parent packet made.

`tools/storybook/sheet-inventory.mjs` regenerates the table from what actually changes —
the sheet-grammar registries (read as data, since the lane launches a browser on import),
the modal producer classes, `story-coverage.mjs --json`, the screenshot manifest and the
reference trees — so the audit stays live. Its count test independently re-greps the
producer classes, opens every cited producer line, states every cited reference directory,
checks every capture id against the manifest, and fails when the committed document drifts
from what the registries produce.

<!-- ANCHOR:decisions -->
## Key Decisions

| Decision | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Rows count surfaces, not modules | The coverage gate counts 40 renderable modules; the app opens more than it tracks. 18 modal producers, toast, the column and bulk-edit menus and the panel renderers are in the table as "untracked" rows — the blindness is now named, which is what later phases will read |
| References are filename matches, never image reads | No eyes were on the captures here; every gap note says what a filename can and cannot support, and the how-to-read preamble states the rule |
| The generator reads the registries as text | Importing `tools/live/sheet-grammar.mjs` launches its Playwright lane; the array literals are plain data, so parsing them keeps the inventory a fast, browserless check |
| Recorded absence over silence | A surface with no capture or no reference gets the literal "none" — 18 and 46 rows respectively — because an unrecorded absence is what let earlier coverage claims drift |

<!-- ANCHOR:limitations -->
## Known Limitations

1. **References are matched by filename, not by eye.** Nobody read the reference images; a gap note marked "filename read only" means the mapping rests on the reference trees' own naming discipline (`notion-ios-<category>-*`, `anytype-mobile-sheet-*`). Any redesigning phase must open the captures it cites before trusting the mapping.
2. **The coverage gate cannot see the sheet producers.** The 40-module census excludes `sort-panel-renderer.ts`, `filter-panel-renderer.ts`, `view-config-panel-renderer.ts`, `toolbar-renderer.ts`, `toast.ts`, the record-detail panel and every DbModal subclass — they open through `createSurfaceShell` and do not match the gate's parent-taking-export shape. They are table rows, not gate rows; 18 primary rows consequently have no capture (the Modal family is outside the storybook stub by its own declaration) and stay "none" until a later phase gives them captures.
3. **The phase-close changelog entry does not exist yet.** The spec's deliverable points at `../changelog/`, which no 005 packet has created; the convention is left to the phase-close, not invented here.
