# Fresh UI review of 0.0.27 against the Anytype references

> Fresh reviewer under D4. Nothing here is self-certified: every finding names the capture it was
> read from, and every number was measured off the PNG with a per-pixel scan (PIL 11.3), not judged
> by eye. Tiers follow `evidence-and-proof.md`: **OBSERVED** is a pixel or a command output,
> **DERIVED** follows from an observation plus a stylesheet read, **INFERRED** is plausible and
> unverified. `origin/main` at `0295e32d` carries four commits past 0.0.27 (the owned-menu submenu
> leg, `fc730ed9`, `746bc4bd`) that this review did not read. The two owned-menu findings below say
> so.

---

## 1. VERDICT

**0.0.27 is not ready for the operator's "debugged, refined, perfected" bar. One P0 and eleven
P1 findings stand, and three of them sit on surfaces the operator has already reported.**

The one P0 is the linked view (046, row 42): the production embed renderer paints an empty 34px
band between every data row and hides its first row behind the header. The root cause is a
stylesheet specificity tie that the embed rules win, so it is a one-rule fix.

The toolbar family (053) shipped its structure correctly. The five primitives, the chip rail, the
value-column settings summary and the declared trigger state all read as designed. What it did not
carry is the 044 grammar onto the phone: the popover shell's sheet header sits at a 10px inset
against the 16px ruling, and the view-tab menu it builds has 30px rows on a phone that owes 44px.

The phone sheet family is in better shape than the operator's last device pass saw. Every sheet
sampled carries a title and a close, and the sort-direction stacked pair dims its parent and lets
its handle peek exactly as 048 specifies. The remaining phone defects are density and inset drift,
not lifecycle.

The board and gantt ports read as faithful to Project Manager. The record panel docks to the pane
at full height as 006 ruled. Neither is blocked here.

What only the operator can confirm: every finding below was read off a headless-Chrome capture
that stands in for Obsidian's theme variables. Where a colour ratio is quoted, the hex is the
harness's stand-in and the device may differ. Those rows say so.

## 2. METHOD

**What was opened.** 61 tracked PNGs under `screenshots/notion-clone/` across all five groups,
both themes and both devices, sampled one per distinct surface with the constructed capture
preferred over the hand-written fixture wherever one exists. 14 Anytype references under
`screenshots/anytype/desktop/{sets,app}/` and `mobile/{sheets,app}/`. Every file named in §3 was
opened and looked at, per `screenshot-currency.md`.

**Currency.** `npm run screenshots:verify` exited 0 at HEAD: *"554 entries match their sources,
and none is blank or identical across themes"* (OBSERVED). So every tracked capture describes the
0.0.27 tree.

**Where a capture was missing, one was mounted.** Twelve captures were taken through the repo's
own `buildRenderAssertionBundle` and `runRenderAssertions`, in headless Chrome via
`playwright-core`, at the capture set's own viewports (1440×900 and 402×874, both at 2×) with the
same three stylesheets the tracked constructed captures load. The driver is
`fable-capture.mjs` in the scratchpad beside the PNGs, under
`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-michelkerkmeester-MEGA-Development-Obsidian-Plugin/e80c6d75-9d5c-4af2-af70-05fb120371b4/scratchpad/fable-review/`.
They cover the toolbar with live filter and sort rules (four files), the view-tab context menu on
both devices, the utilities sheet, and three stacked phone pairs (filter property picker in both
themes, record select value, sort direction). All twelve mounted with the production-render
marker present and zero page errors (OBSERVED, driver output).

**How numbers were read.** Mobile captures are 804px wide for a 402px viewport, so every phone
figure is the pixel ÷ 2. Desktop captures are 2880px for 1440px, ÷ 2 likewise. Insets were found
by scanning a header band for the first pixel that differs from the sheet fill. Contrast ratios
use the WCAG relative-luminance formula on the sampled RGB. Panel widths were read as the extent
that differs from the page background and include the panel's drop shadow, so a reading of 305px
is the 292px `panel` role plus shadow and a reading of 565px is the 552px `condition panel`.

**What this review did not do.** It ran no gate other than `screenshots:verify`, changed no
product code, and read nothing on a device. Row 42's operator screenshots under `scratch/` are
gitignored and were not consulted.

## 5. MISSED PATTERNS

Anytype patterns visible in the reference set that no true-up carries as a decision.

- **The phone toolbar is one row, not three.** Anytype collapses the tab row to a `Grid ⌄` dropdown beside a settings icon and a split `New` (`anytype/mobile/app/anytype-mobile-set-grid-dark.png`, `anytype-mobile-set-kanban-light.png`). Ours stacks tabs plus search, then the trigger cluster, then a floating `+` (`components/constructed-toolbar-mobile-dark.png`). `053` T1 records the dropdown as the phone rung but no packet owns the collapse of the cluster row or the FAB.
- **The phone grid is a two-line card, not a scrolling table.** Every Anytype phone row is a title line over a values line with column labels once at the top (`anytype-mobile-set-grid-dark.png`). Ours scrolls a 45px table row sideways with a drag handle and a checkbox (`views/constructed-table-mobile-dark.png`). No true-up mentions it.
- **Kanban cards show values without labels.** Anytype's card is a title then bare values, one per line (`anytype/desktop/sets/project-tracker/anytype-project-tracker-kanban-light.png`). Ours renders `label: value` rows and a card with fifteen properties runs to 600px (`views/constructed-board-desktop-dark.png`). `045` decides which fields show but not whether the label shows.
- **The split `New` on the phone.** Anytype's phone `New` is a 73×28pt pill split by a 1pt rule (`053` §2 measured it). Ours has no phone `New` control at all beyond the FAB.
- **No handle on an anchored phone menu.** `051` row 26 flipped the `menu` role to a handle-less card, and the owned menu still ships as a grab-handle sheet (`components/constructed-owned-menu-mobile-dark.png`). Recorded, not new, and `origin/main`'s menu leg may already move it.
